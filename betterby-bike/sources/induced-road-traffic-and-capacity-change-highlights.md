## Reported study and results

The highlights describe an analysis of **eight major capacity changes of Budapest bridges** over a **55-year
analysis period**. They report an average elasticity of **0.5** between induced road traffic over **five years**
and capacity change. They also say the elasticity was relatively stable across the analysis period and that
capacity reduction had a similarly strong effect on travel demand (suppressed traffic) as capacity extension.[^highlights]

These are the publication's reported conclusions, not independently checked causal estimates. The screenshot
supplies no calendar years, bridge identities, traffic or capacity definitions, comparison design, adjustment
method, case weights, separate expansion/reduction estimates or uncertainty measures. It does not show whether
missing bridge traffic was diverted elsewhere or whether travel times changed.
