# Barcelona Is Turning Subway Trains Into Power Stations

Natalie Donback, Grist, republished by Next City October 7, 2024.
[Original Grist publication](https://grist.org/transportation/barcelona-is-turning-subway-trains-into-power-stations/).
The five-page PDF was read, with substantive reporting on pp. 1–3 and site material on pp. 4–5.

## Reporting

The article describes MetroCHARGE, which directs recovered subway braking energy to trains, station
amenities and street EV chargers in metropolitan Barcelona. It interviews TMB project director Jordi
Picas, regional sustainable-mobility official Marc Iglesias and City College of New York electrical
engineering researcher Ahmed Mohamed. Four chargers reportedly opened in July, including Bellvitge's,
with three more planned. It reports three inverters installed and 13 in progress, not a completed rollout.[^donback2024]

Picas reports regenerative braking supplies 33% of train energy. TMB expects 41% once the inverter
rollout is complete, with about 3,885 metric tons of annual CO2 savings. The reported €7.8 million
($8.6 million) project cost and expected four-to-five-year recovery through energy savings and charging
revenue are not audited financial results. The article also reports a 1.8°F subway temperature decrease
without a measurement period, comparator or adjustment for other energy-management changes.[^donback2024]

For replication, Picas emphasizes expensive train retrofits, long fleet lifetimes and public-space
permissions for chargers. Barcelona reportedly had regenerative braking on all trains since the 1980s;
MetroCHARGE is not the initial introduction of the braking technology. Mohamed stresses missing
savings data, pilot projects and unresolved stakeholder responsibilities. The MTA did not provide an
interview. The article attributes a potential 35% NYC subway energy reduction to his team's 2018 study,
linking a [wayside-energy-storage white paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/338541009_White_Paper_on_Wayside_Energy_Storage_for_Regenerative_Braking_Energy_Recuperation_in_the_Electric_Rail_System).
That study and the linked TMB materials were not reviewed; the NYC figure is a conditional estimate,
not measured adoption savings.[^donback2024]

## Qualifications and connection

No energy-accounting methods, absolute kWh series, uncertainty intervals or emissions factors are supplied.
The opening's statement that one-third of recovered electricity powers trains has a different denominator
from the later 33% of train consumption figure; they cannot be substituted for one another. The planned
end-of-September completion remains future-tense despite the October republication date. Completion and
realized savings are unconfirmed. Recovered energy is not evidence of new renewable generation, despite
the article's terminology.

This adds a distinct operational-efficiency case, not independent confirmation of the bundle's electric
two-wheeler oil-displacement estimates or evidence of cycling, mode-shift or street-redesign benefits.
