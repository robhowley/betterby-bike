# Best Complete Streets Policies 2025

Smart Growth America, resource page for its 2025 report. The supplied three-page PDF is a capture of
that page, **not the report itself**, despite its title. All captured text was read; substantive content
is on pp. 1–2. The linked [original report](https://www.smartgrowthamerica.org/media/2025/06/Best-Complete-Streets-Policies-2025.pdf)
was identified from the PDF hyperlink but not reviewed. The page gives no publication date.

## Reporting basis and results

The publisher says it reviewed and scored **43 Complete Streets policies passed in 2023 and 2024**.
Its top ten span cities, a parish and a metropolitan planning organization. San Antonio leads at
96/100, followed by Nashville at 89 and Clyde, Ohio at 85. Asbury Park and Red Bank, New Jersey,
rank ninth and tenth at 70 and 69 respectively.[^sga]

The page argues that communities of different sizes can tailor policies to local mobility goals.
It interprets the policies as a path toward safer transportation, more travel options and physical
activity. These are publisher interpretations and anticipated benefits, not outcomes measured in
the captured text. Its warning that communities without policies meeting the criteria will experience
unsafe roads is not supported here by an intervention comparison.[^sga]

## Limits and related evidence

The capture omits scoring criteria, selection methods, individual policy text and the advertised
case studies. Scores cannot be independently reproduced from this page. Neither completed projects,
crashes, cycling participation nor access benefits are measured. The 43 policies are the review set,
not an established census of all U.S. policies.
