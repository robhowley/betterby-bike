- [Creating Accessible, Equitable, Safe, and Complete Networks for Young Pedestrians](2024-creating-accessible-equitable-safe-complete-networks-young-pedestrians.md):

- [Smart Congestion Relief](2023-vtpi-smart-congestion-relief.md): Litman's October 2023 policy synthesis;

- [Curb the Chaos: Solutions for Cities at the Curb](2023-curb-the-chaos-solutions-for-cities-at-the-curb.md):

- [Travel in London, Report 15](2022-tfl-travel-in-london-report-15.md): partial cycling-focused review of TfL's
