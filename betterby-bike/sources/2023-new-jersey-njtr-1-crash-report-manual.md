# New Jersey NJTR-1 Crash Report Manual

[Supplied manual](../../raw-sources/research/new-jersey-njtr-1-crash-report-manual.pdf), revised January 1, 2023,
edition 2.0. The introduction credits a multi-agency New Jersey traffic-records group and describes an
expansion from 149 to 153 report fields. This is administrative guidance, not an injury study or a test of
reporting completeness.[^manual]

## Focused review

Read the cover, introduction and contents, reporting guidance on printed pp. 8–12, reportability and fatal
change-report instructions on pp. 16–17, person coding on p. 51, vehicle definitions on pp. 74–76, and
personal-conveyance guidance and statutory definitions on pp. 127–130. Other sections and form images
were not fully reviewed. Claims below come from readable extracted prose, not a visual audit of the forms.

## Relevant instructions

NJTR-1 excludes pedestrian/pedalcyclist crashes without a motor vehicle in transport. The reporting scope
includes private roads and property, not just public highways. Injury, death or property damage exceeding
$500 to any one person makes a crash reportable; non-reportable reports are not sent to NJDOT for
processing. These rules limit the dataset's coverage of cycling harm but do not quantify missing cases.[^manual]

The manual separates solely pedal-powered bicycles from low-speed electric bicycles and scooters.
Its personal-conveyance instructions classify the latter operators as pedestrians when a qualifying
motor-vehicle crash occurs, with code 17 in vehicle-type boxes 108/109 and the device description in the
narrative. Mopeds are separately reportable under the cited statute. A pedalcyclist-only count therefore
is not automatically an all-bicycle count.[^manual]

## Limitations

These are instructions for the supplied 2023 edition, not verified current law or evidence that officers
consistently applied them. No crash records, trip denominators, implementation audit or later manual were
reviewed. Internal references from p. 75 to definitions on pp. 129–131 do not precisely locate the
personal-conveyance discussion, which appears on printed p. 127 in this capture.

## Extracted findings

- [NJTR-1 coverage excludes cycling crashes without a motor vehicle in transport](/findings/safety/njtr-1-cycling-crash-coverage.md).
- [Low-speed e-bike users are not coded as pedalcyclists](/findings/safety/njtr-1-low-speed-ebike-classification.md).

These refine [cycling injury-risk interpretation](/topics/cycling-injury-risk.md), not estimates of mode safety.
