# Cycling rates in London rose by a quarter over the past five years

Ian Mansfield, ianVisits, November 28, 2024. Read the seven-page
[archived article](../../raw-sources/news/cycling-rates-in-london-rose-by-a-quarter-over-the-past-five-years.pdf),
including the TfL chart visually. Reporting occupies pp. 1–2; later pages contain comments and site furniture.

## Reporting and evidence

Mansfield reports TfL's estimated 1.33 million daily cycle journeys in 2024, 26% above 2019.
The reproduced chart more precisely labels the measure **daily cycle stages**, averaged across a seven-day
week, with central, inner and outer London components. Its note says comparable 2020–2021 data are
missing because of the pandemic. The chart attributes the series to TfL Strategic Analysis, Customer & Strategy.[^mansfield2024]

TfL reportedly expanded the strategic cycle network from 90 km in 2016 to over 400 km in September 2024.
Just over 27% of Londoners lived within 400 metres of it, against a 40% target for 2030. The article
announces construction on C34 from Wood Lane to Shepherds Bush for January and anticipated borough-led
route completions the following year. These are dated plans, not confirmed subsequent delivery.[^mansfield2024]

Walking and Cycling Commissioner Will Norman interprets rising use as evidence that people use the right
infrastructure. The article supplies no design capable of isolating that causal explanation. Reader comments
raise route-location and accessibility arguments but provide no systematic evidence.[^mansfield2024]

## Limits and extracted Finding

The underlying TfL release and calculation methods were not independently reviewed. The article gives no
sampling detail, uncertainty intervals or population-normalized rate. Stages are not unique cyclists or
necessarily complete trips. Rounded chart components sum to 1.32 million in 2024 rather than the prose's
1.33 million; neither rounding nor the precise calculation is explained. Network proximity is not measured
route usability or equitable access. This extends the period covered by existing TfL monitoring, not an
independent confirmation of that evidence.
