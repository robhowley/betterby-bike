## Reporting and attributed explanations

The graphic reports that the MTA reduced fines for toll evaders from $100 to $50. The caption attributes to MTA
spokesperson Joana Flores the explanation that part of the change will align MTA fine amounts with those of the
Port Authority. It quotes no policy text or effective date.[^gothamist]

David Moretti, identified as a former executive vice president of MTA Bridges and Tunnels, says the reduction is
likely intended to encourage more people to pay their fines. This is his interpretation of the policy's intent,
not a measured improvement in collection. The caption also attributes to Gov. Hochul a statement that drivers owe
more than $12.5 million in unpaid tolls and late fees; it supplies no accounting date, period or underlying records.
That amount should not be described as fines alone.[^gothamist]
