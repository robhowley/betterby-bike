# Average Cost of Owning a Car in 2026

Myryah Irby, MoneyGeek, updated September 11, 2026. Edited by Venus Zoleta; reviewed by Nathan Paulus.
[Supplied article PDF](../../raw-sources/news/average-cost-of-owning-a-car-in-2026-11-577-a-year.pdf).

## Reporting basis

This US consumer-finance explainer combines AAA new-vehicle cost estimates with inflation and household-income
comparisons, Experian payment reporting and budgeting advice. Despite the 2026 title, the latest annual costs
are labeled **2025**. The article links [AAA's 2025 fact sheet](https://newsroom.aaa.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/UPDATE-AAA-Fact-Sheet-Your-Driving-Cost-9.2025-1.pdf),
AAA's 2024 sheet, BLS/FRED inflation data, Census 2024 income data and Experian Q3 2025 reporting. These
underlying materials were not reviewed for this ingestion.[^moneygeek2026]

## Main reporting

- A financed new vehicle driven 15,000 miles annually reportedly costs $11,577 a year, about $965 monthly.
  Depreciation is the largest component at $4,334, rather than a monthly cash payment.
- The category table ranges from $8,381 annually for small sedans to $14,781 for half-ton pickups, a $6,400
  difference. Electric vehicles are reported at $10,682, above small sedans and compact SUVs but below medium SUVs.
- MoneyGeek reports a 5.9% nominal and 8.2% inflation-adjusted decline from 2024 to 2025, with real costs about
  10% below 2023 and 2.7% below 2020. This is a different period and measure from NerdWallet's 2024 price index.
- Its ratio of annual new-car cost to estimated median household income falls from 15.1% in 2023 to 13.8% in
  2025. The latter uses an estimated $83,730 income, not an observed 2025 household spending distribution.
- Budget rules, smaller-vehicle recommendations and claimed EV cost-parity conditions are advice or author
  interpretations, not evaluated effects of a transportation intervention.[^moneygeek2026]

## Limits and reconciliation

Read all 14 PDF pages as extracted text, including cost, vehicle-category, income and annual-trend tables.
Collapsed sections and any chart-only series were not accessible in that text; extracted prose and tables support
this account. The article does not supply a reproducible income-projection method, vehicle-level data or uncertainty.
Its annual trend table labels the 2023 nominal increase over the prior year as 27.4%, but its displayed
$10,728 and $12,182 totals imply about 13.6%. Do not use that percentage as a verified year-on-year change.
The rounded annual components sum to $11,578, one dollar above the reported total.[^moneygeek2026]

The bundle already covers [AAA's 2024 estimate](2024-aaa-your-driving-costs.md) and
[NerdWallet's earlier AAA/Experian reporting](2024-nerd-wallet-cost-car-ownership.md). This article adds a later
annual estimate, not independent confirmation of those earlier results. Its reported income share is not the
average share households actually spend, and depreciation-based ownership costs are not interchangeable with
loan repayments or savings from eliminating individual driving trips. No cycling substitution or access benefit
is measured.
