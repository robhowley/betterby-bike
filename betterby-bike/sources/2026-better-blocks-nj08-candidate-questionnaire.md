# Ali and Menendez in NJ-08 Answer the Better Blocks Questionnaire

Eric Conner, Better Blocks New Jersey, May 4, 2026. [Original publication](https://betterblocksnj.org/2026/05/04/ali-and-menendez-in-nj-08-answer-the-better-blocks-questionnaire/).

## Reporting basis and access

Better Blocks presents what it describes as unedited, side-by-side answers from Mussab Ali and
Rep. Rob Menendez Jr., candidates in the June 2, 2026 Democratic primary for New Jersey's eighth
congressional district. This is a candidate questionnaire, not independent policy evaluation or a
representative resident survey. All captured text was read. The 17-page PDF contains complete Q1–Q8
responses, then cuts off during the Q9 prompt on p. 16 and moves to comments and the footer.
Street-safety and parks responses advertised in the introduction are not available in this capture.[^conner2026questionnaire]

## Housing and transit positions

Both candidates support expanding housing tax credits and making housing vouchers an entitlement.
On linking federal transit funding to nearby housing, Ali explicitly favors requirements for walkable,
mixed-income, higher-density development. Menendez supports incentives but qualifies his openness
to funding conditions with tenant protections, affordability and concern about penalizing fiscally
constrained communities. Both call for amendments rather than unqualified passage of the ROAD to
Housing Act as presented in the questionnaire.[^conner2026questionnaire]

Both support bus rapid transit investment on JFK Boulevard and better Elizabeth–Newark transit.
Their environmental-review positions differ: Ali supports categorical exclusions for transit in
existing rights-of-way; Menendez supports targeted streamlining but generally opposes exclusions
that bypass meaningful environmental analysis. On PATH oversight, Ali endorses moving from FRA
to FTA regulation; Menendez is open to evaluating reclassification, subject to labor and safety
consultation, rather than committing to it. Ali endorses Penn Station through-running investment;
Menendez calls for further feasibility assessment and coordination without an unconditional
endorsement. The publisher's support labels should not erase these qualifications.[^conner2026questionnaire]

## Limits and relationship to existing coverage

Candidate predictions of lower costs, less car dependence, more reliable transit and greater rail
capacity are arguments for proposals, not measured outcomes. Legislative histories and regulatory
claims in the questions and responses were not independently checked. The missing remainder
prevents conclusions about either candidate's cycling or street-safety commitments.

This adds dated political positions to the [access synthesis](/topics/active-commuting-and-access.md#housing-finance-proposals-are-not-delivered-access),
complementing [Build HUBS financing coverage](/sources/2026-build-hubs-housing-near-transit.md),
not independently confirming housing or mobility benefits. No new Finding is extracted: these are
policy positions, not a distinct evaluated access or safety result.
