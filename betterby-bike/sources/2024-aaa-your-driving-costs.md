# Your Driving Costs 2024

AAA, 2024 fact sheet. [Original PDF](https://newsroom.aaa.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/YDC_Fact-Sheet-FINAL-9.2024.pdf).

## Scope and method

AAA estimates US new-vehicle personal-transportation costs using five top-selling models in each of nine
categories, standardized over five years and 75,000 miles. Its proprietary methodology uses sources including
Vincentric LLC. The sheet reports a sales-weighted average MSRP of $38,883 for study vehicles, but does not
detail the aggregation weights for every cost estimate.[^aaa2024]

Costs combine depreciation, financing, fuel, insurance, government fees and maintenance/repair/tires. Financing
assumes a five-year loan with 15% down; insurance assumes full coverage for a driver under 65 with more than
six years' experience and no accidents, living in a suburb or city. Fuel prices cover the 12 months ending
May 2024.[^aaa2024]

## Main results

The overall annual ownership-and-operation estimate is $12,297 at 15,000 miles per year, including $4,680
annual depreciation. Average costs per mile are $1.06 at 10,000 annual miles, 82 cents at 15,000 and 70 cents
at 20,000. Category estimates range from 59.24 cents per mile for small sedans to $1.10 for half-ton pickups;
electric vehicles are listed at 84.69 cents, with the lowest maintenance/repair/tire costs but highest
depreciation.[^aaa2024]

## Limits and access

Read extracted text of both pages, including the category tables and methodology. No separate detailed
vehicle breakdown, calculator, underlying dataset or proprietary model was reviewed. These are standardized
estimates, not observed household budgets or cycling-intervention outcomes. Actual costs vary by location,
driving and operating conditions. Depreciation is value loss, not a recurring cash payment; average cost per
mile is not the cost avoidable by replacing one driving mile.

The category table's prose rankings conflict with its numeric costs: it calls subcompact SUVs third-highest
and medium SUVs second-most expensive, although the displayed electric-vehicle cost exceeds both. Rankings
are not used here, and the discrepancy remains unresolved.[^aaa2024]

The [earlier NerdWallet account](2024-nerd-wallet-cost-car-ownership.md) reports AAA's **2023** estimate.
This fact sheet is a later annual estimate, not independent confirmation or a review of the 2023 underlying study.
