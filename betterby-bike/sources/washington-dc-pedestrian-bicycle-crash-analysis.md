# Washington, DC pedestrian and bicycle crash analysis

The [supplied PDF](../../raw-sources/research/washington-dc-pedestrian-bicycle-crash-analysis.pdf) contains only
an image headed “16. Abstract.” The title above is descriptive, not a verified publication title. No authors,
publication date, report identifier or original publication URL are visible.[^dcabstract]

## Study and methods reported

The study digitized, classified and analyzed Washington, DC pedestrian and bicycle crashes from **2012–2014**.
It used NHTSA PBCAT and LMCM crash typologies, examined the top three NHTSA groups in detail and listed applicable
PEDSAFE and BIKESAFE countermeasures. Sparse cases in some original groups prompted proposed new groupings.
CHAID decision trees investigated factors associated with fatal and severe (disabling) injury crashes.
The abstract also says shortcomings in the police crash report forms were discussed.[^dcabstract]

## Reported results

- **68% of crashes occurred at intersections**, apparently across the combined pedestrian/bicycle analysis;
  no mode-specific breakdown or crash count is supplied.
- The leading pedestrian crash type was vehicle left turns with parallel paths; the leading bicycle type was
  “open door to traffic.” The accompanying statement that drivers were at fault or in violation provides no share
  or coding definition.
- For pedestrian severity, the abstract lists traffic control, time, alcohol, speeding, lighting, road type,
  city quadrant and fault. For bicycle severity it identifies bicycle-only crashes and construction-zone crashes.
  It supplies no effect sizes, directions for the individual pedestrian factors, model diagnostics or uncertainty.
  These are reported analytical associations, not established causes.[^dcabstract]

The opening claims of overrepresentation relative to trip shares and higher fatality rates than national averages
lack denominators and numerical comparisons. Listed countermeasures are recommendations, not evaluated effects.[^dcabstract]
