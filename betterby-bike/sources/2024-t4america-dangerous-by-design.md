# Dangerous by Design 2024: Deaths of people walking up 75% since 2010

Abigail Araya, Transportation for America, May 30, 2024.
[Original article](https://t4america.org/2024/05/30/pedestrian-deaths-are-up-by-a-staggering-75-percent-since-2010/).
Read the full five-page PDF capture; substantive reporting is on pp. 2–3. The p. 2 image is a report-cover
photograph, not a data chart. The remaining pages contain navigation and signup material.

## Reporting and evidence

This announcement summarizes Smart Growth America's [Dangerous by Design 2024](https://smartgrowthamerica.org/dangerous-by-design/),
described as combining federal data with lived experience. Transportation for America is a program of
Smart Growth America, so this is the report producer's coverage, not an independent replication.
The underlying report and its methods were not reviewed.

The article reports **7,522 people walking killed** in the latest federal reporting, **75% above 2010**,
and more than 20 deaths per day. Its surrounding discussion compares 2022 with 2021 and says almost every
metro became deadlier. The rankings cover **101 metros** using **2018–2022** fatalities. Memphis is named
as the deadliest, with **344 deaths** in that five-year window, **158 more** than in the preceding five-year
period. Although the article calls Memphis a city, its stated ranking unit is the metro area.[^araya2024]

The article attributes greatest risk to Black and Indigenous Americans, older adults and people walking
in low-income communities, without subgroup counts, rates or comparison denominators. State and congressional
district analyses were still forthcoming at publication.[^araya2024]

## Limits and overlap

The capture supplies no ranking formula, population or walking-exposure denominators, uncertainty estimates,
or controlled design comparison. National death growth is not per-trip risk growth, and the framing about
road design does not identify a treatment effect or cyclist outcome. The same 7,522 national endpoint appears
in [Madison's 20 is Plenty page](/sources/madison-20-is-plenty.md), there compared with 2009 rather than 2010;
these are overlapping national statistics, not independent safety evidence.
