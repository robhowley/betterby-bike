# Before Harlem Blaze that Killed Journalist Fazil Khan

Greg B. Smith, THE CITY, December 30, 2024.
[Original publication](https://www.thecityreporter.nyc/2024/12/30/fdny-marshal-ebike-fire-deaths-amazon/);
[PDF capture](../../raw-sources/news/before-harlem-blaze-that-killed-journalist-fazil-khan-neighbors-complained-about-e-bike-ba.pdf).
The complete substantive article on PDF pp. 1–10 was read; p. 11 is a footer.

## Reporting and evidence

Smith reviewed a dozen FDNY Fire Marshal reports on lithium-ion e-bike battery fires since January 2023,
obtained through public-records requests. Selected fatal cases describe batteries charging near sole exits,
incompatible equipment, residential repair businesses, rapid smoke spread and post-fire reignition.
This is original document-based journalism, not an incidence study or an independent reading here of the
marshal reports. It includes residents' accounts recorded by investigators, FDNY statements and agency responses.[^article]

At 2 St. Nicholas Place, a February 23, 2024 battery fire killed journalist Fazil Khan and injured more than
20 others. At least six tenants told marshals they had complained to management. A former tenant said a
311 complaint concerned e-bikes, but DOT told THE CITY the cited case requested bicycle racks. DOT did not
supply the complaint text or filing date. The article found no evidence of action on battery concerns;
the undisclosed record prevents establishing that DOT received and ignored a charging-hazard complaint.
The building owner's listed head officer did not respond.[^article]

Reported FDNY totals show five battery-fire fatalities in 2024 versus 18 in 2023, while 270 fires through
December 27, 2024 exceeded 268 in all of 2023. Declining fatalities therefore did not mean fewer fires.
The piece also describes federal unsafe-battery legislation being removed from a budget deal and relays
FDNY advice to use compatible chargers. Neither policy effectiveness nor reasons for the fatality decline
were evaluated.[^article]

## Limits and reconciliation

The selected reports give no device, charging-session or rider exposure denominator, no representative
sampling procedure and no comparison of certified versus uncertified equipment. Amazon and eBay did not
immediately respond. The case accounts cannot establish retailer-specific risk or the share of all batteries
that are unsafe. The capture's photo caption dates the Harlem scene February 26; the body dates the fire
February 23. This summary follows the body for the event date.

Existing [CPSC hazard coverage](/findings/safety/cpsc-micromobility-investigated-hazards.md) concerns selected
national investigations for 2017–2022. These later NYC accounts add circumstances and local reporting,
not a replication or a series that can be pooled with CPSC deaths or emergency-department estimates.
