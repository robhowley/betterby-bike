# CHS Circulation Plan

## Publication and basis

Maplewood Township's August 19, 2025 notice announces a Columbia High School drop-off and pick-up
circulation plan beginning September 2, 2025. The [captured notice](../../raw-sources/news/chs-circulation-plan-maplewood-news-maplewood-nj.pdf)
contains the announcement on PDF p. 1 and the South Orange & Maplewood school district's location map
on p. 2; p. 3 contains only a return link. Both substantive pages, including the map legend, were read.
The original webpage URL is not supplied in the extracted text. This is an official implementation
announcement, not a study or post-trial evaluation.[^maplewood2025]

## Announced arrangements

- Drop-offs would be prohibited on both sides of Parker Avenue between Valley and Prospect streets.
  The text names four receiving streets: Valley, Academy, North Terrace and Kensington Terrace.
  The map divides these into six numbered locations, not six different streets.
- Academy's west side between Sherman Place and Parker Avenue and Valley's east side just north of
  Parker are designated drop-off locations. Kensington allows west-side drop-offs from Parker to
  Crowell Place, then both sides to Edgewood Place; North Terrace similarly uses its west side from
  Parker to Franklin Place, then both sides to Edgewood Place.
- Sections of Academy, Beach, North Terrace and Kensington Terrace would become temporarily one-way
  during 7:15–8:15 a.m. and 2:10–3:10 p.m. South Orange would close Hixon Place at Valley Street and
  Sherman Place at Prospect Street. The map shows temporary directions and additional barricade
  positions; it should not be read as a permanent one-way network.
- The trial would last at least six weeks, with adjustments before intended finalization later that
  fall. Additional police officers would help motorists adapt.[^maplewood2025]

## Rationale and limits

Township Committee Member Vic DeLuca describes chaotic school pick-ups and drop-offs and students
crossing against traffic. Improving pedestrian and automobile safety is the stated goal, not an
observed result. The notice calls similar arrangements at Maplewood Middle School and Tuscan Elementary
School successful but supplies no measurements or evaluation methods for that claim.[^maplewood2025]

There are no before/after crashes, crossing conflicts, traffic volumes, travel times, walking or cycling
counts, or resident-access measures. The capture does not establish whether the trial was implemented,
modified or made permanent. It adds a concrete timed school-circulation proposal to
[street-redesign outcomes](/topics/street-redesign-outcomes.md#announced-infrastructure-is-not-a-measured-safety-outcome),
not independent evidence of improved safety or active travel. No new Finding was extracted.

[^maplewood2025]: Maplewood Township, August 19, 2025, PDF p. 1, announcement, official quotation and trial terms; p. 2, “Student Drop-Off Location Map,” numbered locations, directional arrows and closure legend.
