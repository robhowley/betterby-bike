# Average Car Payment and Auto Loan Statistics: 2026

Maggie Gunara, LendingTree, updated September 18, 2026. [Publication website](https://www.lendingtree.com/auto/).
The supplied PDF identifies the article but does not expose its article-specific URL in the recovered links.

## Reporting basis

This US consumer-finance compilation attributes Q2 2026 payment, borrowing and term figures to Experian's
*State of the Automotive Finance Market*, debt and delinquency figures to the New York Fed Consumer Credit
Panel/Equifax, and July price changes to the [BLS CPI release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_08122026.htm).
It also includes LendingTree platform vehicle preferences and commentary from its analyst Matt Schulz.
The underlying datasets and reports were not independently reviewed.[^lendingtree2026]

## Main evidence

- Reported average monthly payments were $765 for new vehicles, $542 for used vehicles and $617 for new leases,
  up 2.1%, 1.9% and 0.7% respectively from Q2 2025. New/used loan amounts averaged $43,610/$27,852;
  terms averaged 69.5/67.9 months, versus 35.9 months for leases.
- Credit-tier differences are not monotonic: nonprime borrowers (601–660) had the highest new-vehicle payment
  ($816) and longest new-loan term (75.6 months), while super-prime borrowers (781–850) averaged $741 and
  64.9 months. Loan amounts also differed, preventing an isolated interpretation as the effect of credit score.
- Outstanding auto debt was reported as $1.713 trillion, 9.1% of consumer debt, with $210.8 billion in Q2
  originations. The share of balances at least 90 days delinquent was 5.5%, down from 5.6% in Q1 but up
  10.0% relative to a year earlier. That stock measure differs from the reported 7.9% transition into
  30-day delinquency; the article does not explain the latter's annualization.
- July new-vehicle prices reportedly rose 0.5% year over year while used prices fell 1.9%. Falling purchase
  prices therefore should not be conflated with the separate quarterly payment comparison.[^lendingtree2026]

## Interpretation and limits

Read all 17 PDF pages as extracted text, including payment, loan and term tables. Charts on pp. 9, 13 and 15
have no recoverable numerical series; only accompanying prose is used. The article supplies no sample sizes,
weighting or uncertainty for the financing averages. Its age-group originations total $210.5 billion rather
than the stated $210.8 billion; the difference is unexplained. Schulz's comments on constrained savings,
car dependence and shopping for loans are advice, not evaluated access effects. LendingTree discloses its
marketing lead-generator role and presents loan offers.[^lendingtree2026]

These are financing measures for financed or leased vehicles, not all-car costs or household budget shares.
They update the period covered by [NerdWallet's earlier Experian reporting](2024-nerd-wallet-cost-car-ownership.md),
not independently confirm it. Neither payment trends nor delinquency establish savings from cycling or the
availability of practical alternatives.
