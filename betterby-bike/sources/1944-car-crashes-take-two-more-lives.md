# Car Crashes Take 2 More Lives

*The News-Palladium*, Benton Harbor, Michigan, January 4, 1944, front page. The photographed Newspapers.com viewer
labels the archive “The Herald-Palladium,” but the printed masthead reads *The News-Palladium*.
[Supplied page photograph (local PDF)](../../raw-sources/news/1944-car-crashes-take-two-more-lives.pdf).[^newspalladium1944]

## Event and reporting basis

The banner headline reports two more deaths from car crashes. The right-hand article is headed “Kalamazoo Man
Killed Today On Territorial,” with a subheading naming James D. Creed and reporting Theodore Eberhardt's death.
The readable opening describes Creed's car striking a tree on Territorial and Eberhardt dying in hospital after
an earlier injury. An accompanying wreck photograph is headed “Car Hits Tree; One Dead, Two Injured.” These are
contemporary newspaper accounts, not an injury study or an independently verified crash reconstruction.[^newspalladium1944]

The article's visible “Two Others Injured” section reports other occupants' injuries and refers to a sheriff's
account. Much of the small print is too blurred for reliable transcription, so individual ages, addresses, exact
crash times, injury details and explanations of the crash are not extracted. The reviewed text does not establish
any cycling or street-redesign connection.[^newspalladium1944]

## Access and interpretation limits

The supplied one-page PDF contains a photograph of a laptop displaying part of the newspaper page, not a complete
legible scan. Text extraction yielded no substantive text. The page image, original embedded image crops and enlarged
article column were inspected visually. The masthead, archive date/page label, headlines and portions of the article
are readable; smaller text is blurred and the article's lower portion is cut off by the displayed page boundary.
The complete article and any continuation were not reviewed. The browser shows Newspapers.com, but not a recoverable
complete image URL; no external archive copy was accessed.[^newspalladium1944]

These reported deaths establish neither an exposure-based risk estimate nor a trend, comparative transport-mode
risk or infrastructure effect. Do not use the headline as a current statistic, or assume the two deaths arose in
the same crash. No standalone Finding is extracted, and this partial historical account does not change the bundle's
Topic conclusions.
