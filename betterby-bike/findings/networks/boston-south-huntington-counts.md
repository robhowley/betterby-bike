# Boston South Huntington fall bicycle counts

## In brief

- The Boston Globe reports that South Huntington Avenue in Jamaica Plain averaged 301 bicycles per day in fall
  2023, up from 228 in 2022 but below its reported high of 369 in 2017.[^dolven2024]
- Boston added bike lanes there in 2023, but these short fall counts do not establish a causal lane effect or
  new cycling. They are not annual daily averages.[^dolven2024]

## Interpretation and limits

These are the Globe's comparisons of municipal counts, not an independently reviewed dataset. Boston counts over a
few days four times a year; these figures use fall observations, not annual average daily traffic. The article gives
no untreated-route comparison, weather adjustment or uncertainty. Count growth can include route diversion and does
not identify new cyclists, former drivers or a causal lane effect.[^dolven2024]
