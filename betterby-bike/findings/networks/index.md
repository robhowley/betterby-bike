# Network findings

- [First/Second Avenue bicycle volumes](nyc-first-second-bicycle-volumes.md): up to 177% growth reported in 2012,
  without separating new cycling from route shifts.

- [Green Lane ridership changes](green-lanes-ridership-changes.md): 21–171% summary route-count increases, with
  current users reporting both route substitution and trips otherwise made by another mode.

- [Copenhagen Farum route](copenhagen-farum-ridership.md): ULI's reported 52% count growth in 2012–14 and separate
  commuter responses, not a net regional mode-shift estimate.
