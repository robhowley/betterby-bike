# Coconut Grove bike-bus events reportedly reduced drop-off cars and doubled bicycle traffic

## In brief

- Streetsblog reports that three 2023–2024 Bike to School Day events combining a bike bus and pop-up lane in Coconut
  Grove, Miami, averaged nearly 30% fewer cars in the school drop-off line and doubled bicycle traffic.
- The article does not define the comparison baseline. These are event observations, not evidence of lasting school-mode
  shift or the lane's isolated effect.[^wilson2025]

## Evidence and limits

The article attributes the results to a BikeSafe case study linked in the reporting, not independently reviewed here.
The intervention supplemented a long-standing elementary-school bike bus with temporary infrastructure. Bicycle traffic
included parent chaperones, families with multiple children and nearby private-school pupils. Doubling traffic therefore
does not mean doubling enrolled students cycling to that elementary school.[^wilson2025]

No absolute counts, count durations, matched comparison days, uncertainty estimates or post-event follow-up are
provided. Fewer cars in one school's drop-off queue need not mean the same percentage reduction in total household
driving. The report cannot separate lane effects from the organized ride, volunteers or special-event participation. The
organizer says earlier rides without infrastructure had not sustained their event participation, but that account is not
a controlled comparison with this intervention.
