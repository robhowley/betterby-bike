# Momentum reports sharp bicycle growth near Cambridge's Garden Street redesign

## In brief

- Momentum's October 2024 account of BCU Labs reports bicycle mode share up 300% and bicycle volumes up over 500% on
  residential streets within half a mile of Cambridge's Garden Street, within four months of project completion.
- The half-mile project combined separated lanes in both directions with one-way motor traffic; missing baselines and
  methods prevent treating these percentages as causal effects, new cyclists or demonstrated reductions in driving.

## Evidence and interpretation

These are reported relative increases, not percentage-point changes or absolute trip totals. The
article attributes them to before/after monitoring by volunteer-led BCU Labs for the Boston Cyclists
Union. It does not specify initial shares, raw volumes, exact counting periods or the denominator
used for the headline bicycle mode share. Later prose refers to bike/car mode share, not all travel
modes. A growing bicycle share can occur without a fall in car counts.[^johnson2024]

Nearby Bluebikes growth is described without a magnitude. The claim that seasonal adjustment makes
the project's ridership impact "nearly quadruple" has no clear comparison base or formula in the
article, so it is not a usable adjusted effect estimate. Neither that claim nor a selected Reddit
account of bicycle adoption is independent confirmation of the count result.[^johnson2024]

## Limits and related evidence

Only secondary coverage was reviewed, not the linked BCU analysis. The article supplies no uncertainty,
reproducible control comparison or adequate weather/season adjustment details. Wider neighborhood
counts may capture more than use of the redesigned segment, but do not rule out route redistribution
or establish citywide new trips. Safety and congestion benefits were not measured.

The [Boston Globe's Cambridge Street counts](/findings/networks/boston-cambridge-street-counts.md)
concern another street and show an initial rise followed by a dip, not the same project or data.
[Green Lane study counts](/findings/networks/green-lanes-ridership-changes.md) likewise illustrate why
local use changes and new cycling are different measures.
