# Royal London's reported e-bike caseload

## In brief

- BBC reported **150 such cases over six months** at Royal London Hospital's orthopaedics unit in
  September 2025, in coverage of e-bike injuries to riders and a child pedestrian.[^bbc2025]
- The report gives no case definition, rider/pedestrian breakdown, comparison-period count or riding
  exposure. It describes local workload, not a measured rise in injury risk or a comparison per trip.[^bbc2025]
- Surgeons describe increasingly severe injuries and associate them with speed, but the article supplies
  no numerical severity comparison or measured speeds to establish that explanation.[^bbc2025]

## What the count supports

The report places six-year-old pedestrian Frida, who needed surgery for tibia and fibula fractures,
among the 150 cases. It also describes an adult rider's tibial plateau fracture after a fall.
The wording does not justify treating all 150 as pedestrians, children, collisions or surgical cases.
Exact collection dates and whether the tally counts distinct patients are unspecified.[^bbc2025]

Patel's description of a substantial departmental burden and Aresti's account of difficult recoveries
are clinician observations. No denominator for the unit's overall activity, resource-cost estimate,
conventional-bicycle comparator or standardized severity scale is supplied. Increasing use, referral
patterns and changing case mix cannot be separated from changing injury risk using this report.
Recommendations for regulation are not measured intervention effects.[^bbc2025]

## Relationship to other evidence

This local journalism is distinct from [US emergency-department injury profiles](/findings/safety/us-micromobility-ed-injury-profiles.md).
Different settings and ascertainment prevent pooling the count with national surveillance or treating
surgeons' observations as independent statistical confirmation of its comparisons. Both require care
in distinguishing outcomes among treated patients from risk while riding; see
[interpreting cycling injury risk](/topics/cycling-injury-risk.md#local-hospital-reporting-does-not-measure-ebike-risk-per-trip).

[^bbc2025]: BBC London, September 11, 2025, PDF pp. 2–3, illustrative injuries; p. 5, six-month count and workload account; pp. 6–7, severity and speed observations. No underlying clinical dataset was provided or reviewed.
