# US pedestrian deaths fell in 2024 but remained well above 2009

## In brief

- Smart Growth America's 2026 overview reports **7,080 US pedestrian deaths in 2024**, **3.9% fewer
  than in 2023**, following declines in two successive years.
- The same count remained **72% above 2009**. Recent improvement and long-term growth can both be true;
  neither comparison measures risk per walking trip or a street-design effect.[^sga2026]

## What the comparison measures

These are national death counts, not fatalities divided by walking distance or trips. The overview
says growth since 2009 exceeded population and vehicle-mile growth, but neither denominator measures
pedestrian exposure. It presents 2024 as higher than every year since 1982 except 2021–2023.
Its constant-decline projection to 2042 is a scenario, not an observed outcome or causal forecast.
The linked full report, underlying federal data and calculation methods were not reviewed.[^sga2026]

The [earlier 2024 announcement](us-pedestrian-death-growth-2010-2022.md) used a 2022 endpoint and
2010 baseline. This later overview updates the same reporting series; it is not an independent
replication, and the two percentage increases should not be compared as if their baselines matched.
No cyclist-specific outcome or controlled intervention comparison is supplied.
