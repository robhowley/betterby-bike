# New Jersey's public crash downloads lacked timely geocoded records in 2024

## In brief

- Rutgers researchers reported in September 2024 that New Jersey's public crash downloads reached only
  2021 and lacked geocoded coordinates, limiting timely location-based analysis despite being downloadable.
- Their July 2024 national review counted public dashboards/maps in 41 states plus DC and public raw
  downloads in 27 states plus DC. Availability did not establish completeness or usability.
- These are historical access limitations, not a measured cycling-injury undercount or evidence that
  opening data reduces crashes.[^article]

## What was available

New Jersey supplied route mileposts, which the authors describe as harder to translate into locations
and potentially less precise than coordinates. Safety Voyager and Numetric offered restricted access,
including access for NJDOT-funded researchers. A May 2024 Children's Hospital of Philadelphia dashboard
provided county-level injury statistics; DVRPC and Hudson County supplied regional dashboards. These
forms of access are not equivalent to timely, statewide, geocoded public crash records.[^article]

The authors contrast near-real-time New Jersey fatal-crash information with unavailable nonfatal cases
in that feed. They report that fatal crashes constitute less than 1% of all reported crashes and argue
that serious nonfatal injuries also matter for safety decisions. This percentage concerns crashes, not
the share of injured cyclists, and the article provides no cycling-specific completeness estimate.[^article]

## Interpretation and limits

The authors reviewed state/DC data access and interviewed agency staff, university researchers and
advocates, but this article supplies neither detailed audit criteria nor interview counts or methods.
Its recommendations for timely geocoding and better public access have no evaluated crash outcome.
Do not use the July 2024 snapshot as a statement of current data availability.

[NJTR-1 coverage exclusions](/findings/safety/njtr-1-cycling-crash-coverage.md) concern what gets recorded;
this Finding concerns access to those records. Fixing access would not itself fix excluded bicycle-only
crashes, device classification or missing exposure denominators. See
[cycling injury risk](/topics/cycling-injury-risk.md) for the broader measurement distinctions.

[^article]: Noland and Younes, September 9, 2024 article, PDF pp. 2–4 and Figure 1 (July 2024).
