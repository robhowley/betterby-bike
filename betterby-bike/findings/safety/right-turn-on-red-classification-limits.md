# Historical right-turn crash shares do not identify red-signal risk

## In brief

- A U.S. FARS excerpt reports about **84 fatal crashes annually in 1982–1992** involving right-turning vehicles
  where right turn on red (RTOR) was allowed, but says the data did not identify whether the signal was red.
  These are not 84 confirmed fatal RTOR crashes per year.[^rtor]
- A separate result reports RTOR crashes as **0.05% of all traffic crashes in four unnamed states**. That share
  lacks years, counts, coding details and turn exposure; it does not measure risk per turn or the effect of an RTOR ban.[^rtor]

## Recorded maneuver is not signal state

The excerpt places the actual annual fatal RTOR crash count between zero and 84, then suggests it may be closer
to zero. The range reflects the missing signal-state distinction, not a statistical confidence interval; the
suggestion about proximity to zero has no supporting analysis in the crop. It cannot establish that RTOR causes
few deaths or that allowing it has no safety cost.[^rtor]

The excerpt also reports **485,104 total fatalities** over 1982–1992 and says below **0.2% of fatalities** involved
a right-turning maneuver at an RTOR-permitted intersection. It supplies an annual fatal-crash count, not the
corresponding number of deaths in those crashes. The fatality percentage cannot be checked by treating crashes
as deaths.[^rtor]

## Crash composition is not exposure-based risk

A second FARS bullet calls the events “fatal RTOR crashes” and reports **44% involving a pedestrian**, **10% a
bicyclist**, and **33% one vehicle striking another**. The wording conflicts with the preceding qualification
that red signals were not identified. These shares describe reported involvement within a fatal-crash category,
not pedestrian/cyclist shares of deaths, risk per crossing, or confirmed on-red crashes. The remaining categories
and their definitions are absent.[^rtor]

The four-state 0.05% share uses all traffic crashes as its denominator. Without how often people turn on red,
exposure of people crossing, an appropriate comparison or full methods, a small aggregate share is not evidence
of a safe maneuver. The one-page crop contains no tables, uncertainty estimates or policy evaluation, and cannot
support present-day rates.[^rtor]
