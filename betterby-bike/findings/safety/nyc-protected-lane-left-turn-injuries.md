# NYC protected-lane intersections had fewer combined left-turn injuries

## In brief

- Across 149 Manhattan protected-lane intersections, combined pedestrian/bicyclist left-turn injuries
  fell from 295 to 250 (15%) and deaths/severe injuries (KSI) from 32 to 15 (53%), comparing three years
  before and after installation.
- These are uncontrolled combined-mode counts, not cyclist-only injury risks or an isolated lane effect;
  through-movement KSI increased from 44 to 51 (16%).[^report]

## Study and results

DOT evaluated 12 projects on six avenues, covering 12 lane miles and installed in 2008–2010, using
NYSDOT data spanning 2004–2013. The evaluation specifically addresses Manhattan one-way approaches.
Projects included turn geometry, signals, turn lanes and typically pedestrian refuge islands, as well
as protected bicycle space.[^report]

| Combined pedestrian/bicyclist outcome | Three years before | Three years after | Reported change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Left-turn injuries, including fatalities | 295 | 250 | −15% |
| All-movement injuries, including fatalities | 904 | 817 | −10% |
| Left-turn KSI | 32 | 15 | −53% |
| All-movement KSI | 111 | 89 | −20% |
| Through-movement injuries | 314 | 329 | +5% |
| Through-movement KSI | 44 | 51 | +16% |

Movement categories can overlap for crashes involving multiple vehicle movements; totals are counted
once rather than obtained by summing movement columns.[^report]

## Limits

No untreated comparison, exposure adjustment or confidence intervals establish causation or risk per
trip. Background trends and other project components could contribute. The adverse through-movement
counts prevent describing improvement in every movement, but also do not establish harm caused by lanes.
The table combines pedestrians and cyclists, so a 53% reduction in cyclist KSI cannot be inferred.

The [2014 Manhattan protected-lane report](/sources/2014-nyc-protected-bicycle-lanes.md) covers overlapping
avenues with different project scope, years and measures. Do not pool these as independent evaluations
or substitute this left-turn KSI reduction for that report's all-user injury or cyclist-risk results.
