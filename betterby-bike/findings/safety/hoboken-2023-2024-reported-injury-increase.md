# Bike Hoboken reports 52% more traffic injuries in 2024 than 2023

## In brief

- Bike Hoboken reports Hoboken traffic injuries rising from **144 in 2023 to 219 in 2024**, a **52% increase**,
  based on its analysis of local police injury records.
- Reported injury increases were **43% for motor vehicle users, 50% for pedestrians and 86% for cyclists**.
  The announcement provides no exposure or underlying mode counts, so these are not increases in risk per trip.

## Evidence and interpretation

The organization says it analyzed all Hoboken Police Department traffic injury records for the two years.
The supplied announcement reports the results but contains no methods or supporting tables. The full linked
report was not reviewed, and the category described as “motor vehicle injuries” is not further defined.[^announcement]

No comparison city, severity breakdown, reporting-stability assessment or travel-volume denominator is supplied.
The totals do not establish a cause, an infrastructure effect or the rate of serious injury. The announcement's
comparison with statewide fatality increases mixes injuries with deaths rather than comparing like outcomes.
Hoboken's [earlier reported four-year zero-death interval](hoboken-reported-four-year-zero-deaths.md), described
in July 2022, does not imply an absence of nonfatal injuries or contradict this later trend.
