# Findings

- [Louisville tolled-bridge traffic](operations/louisville-tolled-bridge-reported-traffic.md): reported local
  crossing decline, not established regional traffic disappearance or a causal toll effect.
- [Toll-road forecast limits](operations/carmageddon-toll-road-forecast-limits.md): secondary M6 counts and
  forecasting research, without transferable error estimates.

- [Carmageddon induced-demand denominators](operations/carmageddon-induced-demand-denominators.md): distinguishes
  overall-driving growth from added-capacity use in secondary book reporting; underlying studies unidentified.

- [Scooter entry and restaurant spending](business/index.md#scooter-entry-and-restaurant-spending): matched
  rider-spending estimates, non-restaurant null results and population/permitted-fleet extrapolation limits.

- [DC crash composition limits](safety/dc-2012-2014-crash-composition-limits.md): abstract-reported 68%
  intersection share and leading crash types, not bicycle-specific exposure-based risk or treatment effects.

- [Non-exhaust PM10 review-share limits](non-exhaust-pm10-review-share-limits.md): secondary source shares with
  distinct denominators, not current local pollution, exposure or intervention effects.

- [Right-turn-on-red classification limits](safety/right-turn-on-red-classification-limits.md): historical FARS
  right-turn counts lack red-signal identification; four-state crash shares lack exposure and policy comparisons.

- [Jersey City quick-build installation limits](design/jersey-city-quick-build-installation-limits.md): undated
  reported delivery counts, not demonstrated safety, cost or speed benefits.

- [Citi Bike commercial-activity proxies](business/index.md#citi-bike-commercial-activity-proxies): positive
  Jersey City associations, weaker Brooklyn results and limits on interpreting scores as receipts or net gains.

- [Hoboken carless households](access/hoboken-carless-household-comparison.md): undated household prevalence
  comparisons, not commute-mode shares or measured green-infrastructure benefits.

- [Green Line LRT adjacent-road traffic](operations/green-line-lrt-reported-traffic-reduction.md): reported adjusted
  volume reduction and attenuation, with conclusion-only access and no measured delay or citywide driving effect.

- [Boston two-stage queue-box use limits](design/boston-two-stage-queue-box-use-limits.md): abstract-only
  noncompliance reporting, not a quantified usage rate or safety effect.

- [Vehicle visibility measurements](safety/index.md#vehicle-visibility-measurements): six nameplates' near-field
  ground visibility losses, mixed outer-band results and limits on inferring human detection or crash risk.

- [Light-truck design synthesis](safety/index.md#light-truck-design-synthesis): secondary front-end fatality
  associations, turning-crash denominator limits and mixed historical/EV compatibility evidence.

- [New Jersey report-card safety denominator](safety/new-jersey-report-card-safety-denominator.md): fatalities
  per bike commuter differ from travel-exposure risk and category points; observation years are unspecified.

- Youth planning: [Philadelphia crash-network coverage](safety/philadelphia-youth-crash-network-coverage.md)
  distinguishes injury-crash locations from exposure-based risk;
  [PBIC design guidance](design/pbic-youth-design-guidance-limits.md) does not establish measured youth safety or equity
  benefits.

- [U.S. micromobility ED comparisons](safety/index.md#us-micromobility-ed-comparisons-20192022):
  population-based trends, mixed diagnostic profiles and clinical-note missingness, using overlapping NEISS data.

- [CPSC micromobility findings](safety/index.md#cpsc-national-micromobility-surveillance): national ED trends,
  incomplete fatality reports, selected product hazards and scooter survey limits, not exposure-based risks.

- [Access](access/index.md): household-income and vehicle-availability commuting patterns, income-restricted
  housing, bicycle access and low car-parking provision, plus Los Angeles library proximity to protected infrastructure;
  reported U.S. new-car costs and car-cost inflation provide context, not measured cycling savings; New Jersey's
  reported June 2025 e-bike requirements distinguish legal categories and local restrictions, not current law or access gains;
  Boone commentary adds a bounded pedestrian medical-benefit account, not verified rideshare coverage or treatment effects;
  London's cycling surveys add differing demographic profiles, not interchangeable participation rates.
- [Business](business/index.md): retail/food-service sales and employment, economic measurement limits, business-visit
  responses, customer spending, merchant mode-share errors, conversion forecasts, adverse sales reports,
  parking-space revenue, employer accounts, property values and development accounts, plus later Bloor merchant
  reports, Delaware path proximity values and project jobs per dollar, plus mixed San Francisco sales comparisons
  and Los Angeles merchant/customer arrival denominators.
- [Networks](networks/index.md): bicycle volumes including Broadway's reported growth, limits on interpreting
  ridership growth, New Zealand's reported cycling-time decline, ACS commuting measures and Copenhagen's reported
  work-cycling and trip-purpose shares, plus 13-city protected construction shares, Pittsburgh's reported connectivity
  and mixed Boston fall corridor counts; Washington mileage demonstrations add method-dependent King County estimates,
  nonrandom sampling and temporal-adjustment limits, not reliable statewide travel baselines; London's mixed 2017
  indicators distinguish trips, distance and cordons, while Mini-Holland panel associations add weekly active-travel
  changes with mixed participation and attitude results, not isolated lane effects; NJTPA's trip-potential scores
  rank spatial planning conditions, not observed trips or validated demand forecasts.
- [Operations](operations/index.md): travel times, emergency-access accounts, Iowa responder perceptions and response-rate
  estimates, NYC's reported fire-company response-time increase, measurement limits, driving/walking perceptions
  and combined street interventions; historical capacity-reduction reporting adds unclear traffic-disappearance
  denominators and a short-term Hammersmith commuter survey, not a general closure effect; Litman's congestion
  synthesis adds intensity-versus-commuter-burden limits and a secondary DC bikeshare result, not a bike-lane effect;
  Baku-informed bus-lane simulations add mixed scenario comparisons, with passenger/vehicle weighting and model
  inconsistencies, not observed installation effects; an
  [unidentified induced-traffic excerpt](operations/induced-traffic-excerpt-reported-ranges.md) adds reported
  lane-mile/traffic-growth ranges without identifiable studies or a measured congestion effect;
  [Budapest bridge highlights](operations/budapest-bridge-capacity-traffic-highlights.md) add a reported five-year
  elasticity, not an independently verified symmetric capacity response.
- [Design](design/index.md): behavior, local traffic-calming speeds, comfort, public support and historical
  green-pavement approval conditions, plus Copenhagen's reported perceived-safety change; NJDOT's historical
  traffic-calming guidance adds treatment-specific cautions and bicycle-space/merge instructions, not measured effects.
- [Safety](safety/index.md): collisions, injuries, short conflict observations and exposure-based risk, including
  overlapping DC scooter–bicycle comparisons of injury profiles and estimated single-ED visits per citywide mile;
  Streetsblog's reported 2024 pedestrian injury counts by vehicle category without exposure or data definitions, plus
  Clarke's unadjusted New Zealand cyclist/pedestrian fatality calculation and assumption-based premature-death estimate,
  secondary reporting of a 12-city all-road-user casualty association and NYC DOT's 2023 all-user/senior-pedestrian
  serious-casualty claims without supporting analysis; incomplete North Jersey pedestrian death counts and
  Street Smart program accounts with pandemic confounding; U.S. vehicle-weight associations with own/partner-vehicle
  deaths and an untested fleet-downsizing estimate, not cyclist or per-mile risk; partial New Jersey enforcement/crash
  reporting without a crash baseline or isolated enforcement effect, plus a distinct state/national traffic-death
  comparison with no explicit year or baseline; a 2025 New Jersey snapshot separates 350 reported deaths through
  August 18 from a 648-death annual forecast with no stated method; DC scooter injury severity and fall/non-rider
  mechanisms describe hospital cases, not facility-specific injury risk per trip; U.S. national pedalcyclist
  statistics add traffic-fatality counts, early 2020 estimates and noncomparable injury sampling periods.

- [2022 recovery and measurement](networks/london-2022-cycling-recovery-measurement.md): growth with revised
  distance levels and pandemic sampling limits, not an infrastructure effect.
- [2021/22 cyclist profiles](access/london-2021-cycling-profile-denominators.md): weekly composition and annual
  participation do not supply interchangeable measures of inclusion.
- [2021 casualty counts](safety/london-2021-cyclist-casualty-counts.md): adverse cyclist trends despite lower
  all-user pre-pandemic KSI comparisons, without matched exposure-based risk.

- [2021 ridership recovery](networks/nabsa-2021-ridership-recovery.md): annual trip recovery lagged fleet expansion.
- [Mode replacement](networks/nabsa-mode-replacement-survey-limits.md): mixed-year surveys describe walking,
