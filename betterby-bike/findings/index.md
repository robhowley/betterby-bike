# Findings

- [Access](access/index.md): household-income and vehicle-availability commuting patterns, income-restricted
  housing, bicycle access and low car-parking provision, plus Los Angeles library proximity to protected infrastructure;
  reported U.S. new-car costs and car-cost inflation provide context, not measured cycling savings.
- [Business](business/index.md): retail/food-service sales and employment, economic measurement limits, business-visit
  responses, customer spending, merchant mode-share errors, conversion forecasts, adverse sales reports,
  parking-space revenue, employer accounts, property values and development accounts, plus later Bloor merchant
  reports, Delaware path proximity values and project jobs per dollar, plus mixed San Francisco sales comparisons
  and Los Angeles merchant/customer arrival denominators.
- [Networks](networks/index.md): bicycle volumes including Broadway's reported growth, limits on interpreting
  ridership growth, New Zealand's reported cycling-time decline, ACS commuting measures and Copenhagen's reported
  work-cycling and trip-purpose shares, plus 13-city protected construction shares, Pittsburgh's reported connectivity
  and mixed Boston fall corridor counts.
- [Operations](operations/index.md): travel times, emergency-access accounts, Iowa responder perceptions and response-rate
  estimates, NYC's reported fire-company response-time increase, measurement limits, driving/walking perceptions
  and combined street interventions.
- [Design](design/index.md): behavior, local traffic-calming speeds, comfort, public support and historical
  green-pavement approval conditions, plus Copenhagen's reported perceived-safety change.
- [Safety](safety/index.md): collisions, injuries, short conflict observations and exposure-based risk, plus
  Clarke's unadjusted New Zealand cyclist/pedestrian fatality calculation and assumption-based premature-death estimate,
  secondary reporting of a 12-city all-road-user casualty association and NYC DOT's 2023 all-user/senior-pedestrian
  serious-casualty claims without supporting analysis.

## Economic valuation

- [Copenhagen cycling values](copenhagen-cycling-economic-values.md): reported 4.80 kroner per bicycle kilometre,
  or 10.09 when replacing a car journey, without calculation methods or measured project returns.
