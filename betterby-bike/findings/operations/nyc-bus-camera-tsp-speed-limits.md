# NYC camera and TSP speed attribution

## In brief

- Hook and Weinstock found no visible speed benefit in 15 NYC bus-route camera comparisons through October 2024; a
  separate B26 series stayed near 6.1 mph.
- They identify overlapping SBS and transit signal priority (TSP) changes in earlier evaluations, so reported gains
  cannot all be attributed to signals alone.
- Uncontrolled route trends and short follow-up do not show that cameras or TSP have no causal benefit.[^hook2025]

## Camera comparisons

The report describes camera deployment on 15 routes in June 2024, warnings for two months, and citations
from August. It compares peak-period speeds in October 2023 and March 2024 against October 2024;
most routes' latest observation was the slowest. B26, with cameras installed in September 2023, supplies
a longer series fluctuating near 6.1 mph. The executive summary's 16 routes combines these analyses,
not 16 identically observed treatment cases.[^hook2025]

Traffic changes, seasonality and enforcement implementation are not controlled. The report's preceding
June 2024 agency summary describes 623 camera-equipped buses on 14 routes and expanded violations,
while its analysis describes 15 newly equipped routes. Without route-level deployment histories these
accounts cannot establish a uniform start of enforcement exposure. Delayed driver response and legally
permitted lane obstructions are proposed explanations, not measured mechanisms. Citations issued are
not a speed-effect estimate.[^hook2025]

## Signal-priority attribution

The report's critique of NYC DOT's 2017 *Green Means Go* notes that M15's April 2010–May 2014 comparison
spans both October 2010 SBS introduction and 2012 TSP. Bx41's comparison likewise spans SBS in 2013
and TSP in 2015; the authors report similar gains in a separate pre-TSP SBS evaluation. This supports
an attribution warning, not their stronger claim that a corrected analysis would necessarily show zero
TSP benefit. B44 implementation/comparison details conflict, and the authors lacked enough data to
independently corroborate S79 results.[^hook2025]

Their B35 route-speed series, August 2018–December 2019, continues declining after July 2019 TSP
installation on Church Avenue and October bus-lane installation. The route extends beyond the treatment;
other conditions could have worsened. No untreated comparison establishes what speeds would otherwise
have been. Underlying DOT evaluations were not independently read for this ingestion.[^hook2025]
