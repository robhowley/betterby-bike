# Capital Bikeshare congestion reduction reported by Litman

## In brief

- Litman's 2023 synthesis attributes **4% or more lower traffic congestion** in Washington, DC to Capital Bikeshare
  stations, compared with congestion intensity that would otherwise occur, citing Hamilton and Wichman (2016).
- This is a secondary study result, reportedly largest in congested areas, not a measured effect of bike lanes or
  a 4% citywide traffic-volume reduction. The primary study was not read.[^litman]

## Evidence available

The report says the study used a fine-grained traffic dataset, but supplies no observation period, station/road
sample, congestion formula, comparison construction, controls or confidence interval. Its counterfactual wording
must not be converted into a simple before/after count change, minutes saved, or an effect per station.

The reference identifies Timothy Hamilton and Casey Wichman, *Bicycle Infrastructure and Traffic Congestion:
Evidence from DC's Capital Bikeshare*, Resources for the Future (2016), linking
[RFF-DP-15-39-REV.pdf](https://www.rff.org/files/document/file/RFF-DP-15-39-REV.pdf). This identifies the underlying
study, not an independently reviewed second source or confirmation.[^litman]

## Boundaries

Litman's nearby school and shopping examples assume shifts in car trips; they are not additional empirical tests.
His separate cycling-conditions argument allows significant delay on narrow, congested roads where faster traffic
cannot pass, and turning delays even when cyclists have dedicated space. Those are author explanations, not
subgroup estimates from the DC study.

The reported finding cannot establish a typical congestion benefit from any cycling investment. It differs from
[NABSA's survey-based mode replacement](/findings/networks/nabsa-mode-replacement-survey-limits.md), which does not
measure observed traffic reduction, and from [congestion per commuter](/findings/operations/vtpi-congestion-measurement-limits.md).
See [Evaluating street redesign outcomes](/topics/street-redesign-outcomes.md).

[^litman]: Litman, *Smart Congestion Relief*, supplied 6 October 2023 report, p. 51, “Active Modes (Walking
    and Cycling)”; p. 73, Hamilton and Wichman reference. Page 51 visually checked; underlying paper not read.
