# Operations findings

- [Louisville tolled-bridge traffic](louisville-tolled-bridge-reported-traffic.md): secondary crossing-count decline,
  with possible diversion, not measured regional traffic disappearance.
- [Toll-road forecast limits](carmageddon-toll-road-forecast-limits.md): M6 counts and attributed forecasting
  shortfalls lack comparable study details or an isolated pricing effect.

- [Carmageddon induced-demand denominators](carmageddon-induced-demand-denominators.md): reported 2% driving
  growth per 10% more capacity differs from 40%/100% of added capacity used; partial secondary evidence.

- [Budapest bridge traffic elasticity](budapest-bridge-capacity-traffic-highlights.md): highlights report a five-year
  elasticity of 0.5 across eight changes, without methods or separate expansion/reduction estimates.

- [Reported induced-traffic ranges](induced-traffic-excerpt-reported-ranges.md): 3%–6% and later 6%–10% traffic
  growth per 10% more metropolitan lane miles in an unidentified excerpt, not verified causal or congestion estimates.

- [Green Line LRT reported traffic reduction](green-line-lrt-reported-traffic-reduction.md): adjacent-road AADT
  reduction of 18%, weakening from 22% to 16%; conclusion-only access, not a delay or network-wide driving result.

- [Mixed modeled bus-lane time comparisons](baku-bus-lane-modeled-time-comparisons.md): favorable and adverse
  two-lane scenarios, not observed installation effects or transferable thresholds.
- [Bus-lane model measurement limits](baku-bus-lane-model-measurement-limits.md): passenger/vehicle weighting,
  extrapolation, above-limit speeds and unvalidated predictions constrain reuse of the Baku-informed model.

- [Congestion measurement limits](vtpi-congestion-measurement-limits.md): historical city rankings change with
  the commuter denominator; intensity does not measure total burden or cycling's effect.
- [Capital Bikeshare congestion report](dc-capital-bikeshare-reported-congestion.md): secondary 4% or greater
  reduction claim without primary methods, not a bike-lane effect or citywide traffic-volume reduction.

- [NYC loading-zone double parking](nyc-loading-zone-double-parking-report.md): reported 73% reduction at some
  pilot sites, without site counts, baselines or a citywide program effect.

- [Reported traffic disappearance](kruse-capacity-reduction-traffic.md): a secondary 60-case review account's 25%
  average lacks defined denominators; local and wider-area reductions differ and some projects may show no reduction.

- [Hammersmith commute adaptation](hammersmith-bridge-reported-commute-change.md): reportedly 21% of surveyed former
  bridge car commuters stopped driving to work, without sample size, mode shares or measured congestion changes.

- [Hoyt Avenue travel times](nyc-hoyt-avenue-travel-times.md): NYCDOT reported a 51% northbound improvement alongside
  bicycle lanes, signal changes and turn bans, with the calculation unspecified.

- [Green Lane driving/walking perceptions](green-lanes-driving-walking-perceptions.md): mostly unchanged reported
  driving times, parking concerns and mixed pedestrian experiences, not measured delay or injury effects.

- [Manhattan protected-lane traffic performance](nyc-pbl-traffic-performance.md): level taxi speeds and shorter
  Columbus/Eighth Avenue travel times in specific observation windows, without isolated lane effects.

- [Ocean City emergency passage](ocean-city-road-diet-emergency-access.md): reported easier access through a center
  turn lane and shoulder space, without timed response or quantified safety outcomes.

- [Woodbury emergency response times](woodbury-road-diet-response-times.md): a police account reports unchanged
  response times and easier, safer travel, not a measured time saving.

- [NYC fire-company response-time trend](nyc-fdny-response-time-trend.md): reported 25-second increase from fiscal
  2015, with tentative congestion attribution and competing accounts, not an isolated bike-lane effect.

- [Cedar Rapids emergency response rates](cedar-rapids-road-diet-response-rates.md): no significant adjusted
  before/after change at four conversions, with uneven site samples and no untreated controls.

- [Iowa responder perceptions](iowa-road-diet-responder-perceptions.md): unchanged, slower and faster perceived
  response times, with mixed safety and yielding experiences rather than measured delays.

- [Cedar Rapids measurement limits](cedar-rapids-response-measurement-limits.md): inferred routes, alarm-to-arrival
  timing, whole-trip exposure and conflicting printed sample counts and confidence bounds.
