# Nearly 77% of surveyed Coconut Grove parents supported a permanent pop-up-lane route

## In brief

- Streetsblog reports that nearly 77% of parents surveyed after a Coconut Grove school bike bus supported making its
  pop-up lane permanent.
- No survey size, response rate or pre-event attitudes are supplied. The result does not establish an increase in
  support, neighborhood-wide opinion or permanent infrastructure delivery.[^wilson2025]

## Evidence and limits

The reporting concerns BikeSafe's three 2023–2024 Bike to School Day events at a Miami elementary school. The article
links a planning-journal case study but supplies no questionnaire or sampling method; the study was not independently
reviewed. Organizer Kurt Kaminer interprets the events as overcoming opposition to bicycle infrastructure. Without
baseline attitudes or a comparison group, that interpretation is not a measured persuasion effect.[^wilson2025]

The article explicitly notes that the experiment did not immediately catalyze a public campaign for a permanent path.
Kaminer attributes that partly to an already relatively safe route and pending nearby development, and says the
short-lived installation escaped many neighbors' notice. These explanations and his expectation of continued demand are
interview interpretations, not evaluated mechanisms or later outcomes.
