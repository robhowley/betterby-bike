# Jersey City quick-build installation counts do not measure benefits

## In brief

- An undated Jersey City strategy excerpt reports **20 miles of protected bike lanes**, **679 speed humps**,
  **183 locations prohibiting right turns on red** and **14 intersections with LPIs** installed in unspecified
  “recent years.” These are reported delivery counts, not proposed targets or dated current totals.[^excerpt]
- Its endorsement of paint and temporary materials as a low-cost, quick approach is not supported in the supplied
  crop by measured costs, delivery times or safety outcomes, or a comparison with conventional construction.[^excerpt]

## What the excerpt supports

The narrative describes using opportunities such as repaving to install safety projects without moving curbs or
breaking ground. The list documents the scale the author reports, but does not identify installation dates,
locations, counting rules, protected-lane mileage conventions or which treatments were temporary. It does not
measure connected network coverage, cycling use or injury risk.[^excerpt]

## Access and inference limits

Only one cropped image was supplied. Its references 8 and 9 are missing, and the original publication, author and
date are unidentified. An annotation icon partly obscures two additional installation labels; those entries are
not needed for this finding. No baseline, control group or evaluation method is visible. The claim that quick
installations lead to quicker results is the excerpt's rationale, not a demonstrated causal effect. Absence of
analysis in this crop does not establish absence in the full report.[^excerpt]
