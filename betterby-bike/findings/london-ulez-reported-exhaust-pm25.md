# Outer London's reported exhaust PM2.5 reduction after Ulez expansion

## In brief

- Guardian coverage of a GLA report estimates outer-London vehicle-exhaust PM2.5 emissions in 2024
  were 31% lower than without the August 2023 Ulez expansion.[^taylor2025]
- This is an estimated exhaust-emission comparison against a hypothetical no-expansion scenario,
  not a 31% fall in ambient particle concentrations, personal exposure or illness. The primary
  methods and uncertainty were not available in the reviewed article.

## Scope and limits

The report covers the first year of the London-wide expansion and reportedly received extensive
independent expert review. The article does not provide absolute emission masses, model assumptions,
uncertainty intervals or a way to isolate cycling or reduced driving as the mechanism. Exhaust PM2.5
excludes tyre, brake and road-wear emissions. A cleaner exhaust fleet does not itself establish reduced
non-exhaust pollution or a measured street-redesign benefit.[^taylor2025]

The article's other percentages use different outcomes and baselines. Its 99% figure counts monitoring
sites with improved air quality since 2019, not the magnitude of improvement. Its 27% roadside NO2
claim is worded as a decline since 2019, whereas later Guardian coverage describes the prior-year GLA
result against a no-Ulez scenario. That unresolved wording must not be used to convert either result
into an independent temporal trend or confirmation of this PM2.5 estimate.
