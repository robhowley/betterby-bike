# West Hollywood robot-fee sidewalk funding

## In brief

- Next City reported in May 2026 that West Hollywood earmarked delivery-robot advertising revenue,
  program fees and fines for accessibility improvements, including **$4 per device per day** in
  advertising revenue sharing from Serve and Coco.[^article]
- A December report projected **$40,000–$80,000 per year**, but the new model was only three months old.
  This is projected funding, not collected revenue or evidence of improved pedestrian access.[^article]

## Arrangement and evidence limits

City associate planner Paige Portwood described a revised agreement adding annual fees and penalties
for ADA and geofencing violations to a pilot begun in 2020. The article gives no device-day forecast,
revenue breakdown, administrative cost or completed repair count for this fund. It does not establish
whether fees cover program costs, how much infrastructure they can buy or whether they change operator
behavior. The underlying agreement and December report were not separately reviewed.[^article]

A separate Los Angeles account describes Coco identifying three missing Venice curb ramps and working
through a council office to secure city installation. That is not a result of West Hollywood's fund and
should not be counted as evidence that its projected revenue has delivered repairs.[^article]
