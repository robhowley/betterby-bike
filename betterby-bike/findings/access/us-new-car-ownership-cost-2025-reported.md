# MoneyGeek reports 2025 new-car ownership costs of $11,577 annually

## In brief

- MoneyGeek's September 2026 AAA-based explainer reports **2025** US financed-new-car ownership costs of
  $11,577 annually, about $965 monthly, at 15,000 miles a year. This includes depreciation, not just cash bills,
  and is not an average for all vehicles or households.[^moneygeek2026]
- At the same annual mileage, its category table puts small sedans at $8,381 and half-ton pickups at $14,781,
  a $6,400 annual gap. Neither that comparison nor the full ownership total measures savings from cycling
  instead of driving.[^moneygeek2026]

## What is included

The reported annual components are depreciation $4,334; insurance $1,694; fuel $1,950; maintenance/repairs
$1,656; finance charges $1,131; and registration/license/taxes $813. These rounded entries sum to $11,578,
one dollar more than the headline. Depreciation assumes five years and 75,000 miles; financing assumes a
five-year loan and 15% down. Fuel is reported at 13 cents per mile and maintenance at 11.04 cents per mile.
Depreciation is lost vehicle value rather than a recurring cash payment.[^moneygeek2026]

Other reported category totals are $9,591 for hybrids, $10,280 for compact SUVs, $10,682 for electric vehicles
and $12,584 for medium SUVs. EVs therefore are not the lowest-cost category in this table; these are category
averages, not matched models or proof that powertrain alone causes the differences.[^moneygeek2026]

## Limits

Only MoneyGeek's secondary reporting was reviewed here, not the linked AAA 2025 fact sheet. Vehicle-level
inputs, aggregation weights and uncertainty intervals are not supplied. Actual costs depend on the vehicle,
location, driver and use. Fixed ownership costs do not disappear with each avoided trip; the average total is
neither a marginal cost per mile nor demonstrated savings from giving up a car. Loan principal repayments
cannot simply be added to this depreciation-based total. The article contains no bicycle-cost comparison or
assessment of whether destinations can be reached without a car.[^moneygeek2026]
