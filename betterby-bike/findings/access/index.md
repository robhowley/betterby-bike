# Access findings

- [Household income and active commuting](us-household-income-active-commuting.md): highest reported walking and
  bicycle commute rates among workers in U.S. households earning below $10,000 in 2008–2012.
- [Vehicle availability and active commuting](us-vehicle-availability-active-commuting.md): higher walking and
  bicycle commute rates without an available vehicle, without establishing causation or adequate travel choices.

- [Silver Moon Lodge low-parking housing](silver-moon-lodge-low-parking-housing.md): reported high occupancy in
  income-restricted Albuquerque apartments with bicycle amenities and 23 car spaces for 154 units, without measured
  household savings.
