# Access findings

- [Hoboken carless-household comparison](hoboken-carless-household-comparison.md): reported 15.7% versus
  13.7% in Hudson County and 12.5% in North Jersey, with unknown data year and no measured travel or access benefit.

- [London's 2021/22 cyclist profiles](london-2021-cycling-profile-denominators.md): women were 34% of past-week
  cyclists; separate annual ethnicity participation results and changed survey methods use different comparisons.

- [NABSA's user representation gaps](nabsa-user-representation-gaps.md): city-average income, age, race and gender
  composition gaps in selected user surveys, not population participation rates or equity-program effects.

- [London cycling survey profiles](london-cycling-survey-profile-limits.md): differing household/attitudes ethnicity
  estimates and low-income rider composition, not directly comparable population cycling rates or benefit distributions.

- [Household income and active commuting](us-household-income-active-commuting.md): highest reported walking and
  bicycle commute rates among workers in U.S. households earning below $10,000 in 2008–2012.
- [Vehicle availability and active commuting](us-vehicle-availability-active-commuting.md): higher walking and
  bicycle commute rates without an available vehicle, without establishing causation or adequate travel choices.

- [Silver Moon Lodge low-parking housing](silver-moon-lodge-low-parking-housing.md): reported high occupancy in
  income-restricted Albuquerque apartments with bicycle amenities and 23 car spaces for 154 units, without measured
  household savings.

- [Los Angeles library proximity](los-angeles-library-protected-bike-proximity.md): fewer than one-fifth of libraries
  within a quarter mile of protected cycling infrastructure, without measured usable routes or equitable access.

- [AAA's 2023 new-car ownership estimate](us-new-car-ownership-cost-2023.md): reported $12,182 annual cost at
  15,000 miles, distinct from monthly cash expenses and unmeasured cycling savings.
- [NerdWallet's car-cost inflation](us-nerdwallet-car-cost-inflation.md): reported 38% growth since April 2019 in
  a June 2024 snapshot, not a household budget or current inflation estimate.

- [New Jersey's reported June 2025 e-bike requirements](new-jersey-2025-e-bike-requirements.md): low-speed exemptions
  versus Class 3 requirements, with municipal restrictions; historical reporting, not current legal verification or
  measured access gains.

- [Reported pedestrian PIP coverage in Boone](new-jersey-boone-pedestrian-pip.md): an uninsured pedestrian struck
  by a passenger-carrying Lyft driver, as described in legal commentary; not verified law or a universal coverage rule.
