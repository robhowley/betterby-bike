# AAA estimated 2024 new-car costs at $12,297 annually

## In brief

- AAA's 2024 US new-vehicle estimate is $12,297 annually under five-year, 75,000-mile ownership assumptions,
  including $4,680 a year in depreciation. It is not an average for all existing vehicles.[^aaa2024]
- Average ownership-and-operation costs are $1.06 per mile at 10,000 annual miles, 82 cents at 15,000 and
  70 cents at 20,000. These are total-cost averages, not savings from replacing a driving mile with cycling.[^aaa2024]

## What the total contains

The fact sheet estimates five top-selling models in each of nine categories. Besides depreciation, it reports
annual finance costs of $1,332, insurance of $1,715 and license/registration/taxes of $815, plus 14.90 cents
per mile for fuel and 10.13 cents for maintenance/repair/tires. Financing assumes a five-year loan with 15%
down; the insurance profile is an experienced, accident-free driver under 65 with full coverage.[^aaa2024]

Fuel prices cover the year ending May 2024, with regular gasoline at $3.999 per gallon and electricity at
15.8 cents per kWh. These historical inputs are not current prices.[^aaa2024]

## Interpretation and limits

AAA uses proprietary standardized estimates with data including Vincentric LLC, not a representative survey
of household spending. The fact sheet supplies neither uncertainty intervals nor the full vehicle-level
calculation. Actual costs vary by vehicle, location and use.[^aaa2024]

Depreciation represents lost value rather than a monthly cash bill. Spreading ownership costs over fewer
miles raises the average per-mile figure; it does not mean a household spends more in total simply by driving
less. Nor does the full per-mile average measure the savings available while retaining a vehicle. The sheet
provides no bicycle-cost comparison, relinquishment study or evidence that safe alternatives are available.
