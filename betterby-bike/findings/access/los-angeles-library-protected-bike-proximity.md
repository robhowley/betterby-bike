# Fewer than one-fifth of Los Angeles libraries were near protected cycling infrastructure

Urban Institute's February 2022 article reports that **fewer than one-fifth of Los Angeles libraries** were within
**a quarter mile of protected cycling infrastructure**. It uses this example to illustrate remaining gaps in
access to libraries and their community resources.[^urban2022]

The denominator is libraries, not residents, cyclists or library trips. The article does not give the number of
libraries, observation date, infrastructure inventory, distance-calculation method or route-quality assessment.
Being near a segment does not establish a continuous protected route from someone's home, and being farther away
does not establish that a library is unreachable by bicycle. No library-use, health or demographic distribution
outcome is measured in this example. The underlying report was not separately reviewed.

The authors recommend investment in communities with more residents of color and low-income families, resident-led
route decisions, and classes and purchasing assistance. Those recommendations respond to possible access barriers
and gentrification concerns; the proximity statistic does not establish their effects.[^urban2022]
