# US vehicle payments reportedly averaged $765 new and $542 used in Q2 2026

## In brief

- LendingTree reports Experian's Q2 2026 US monthly averages of $765 for new vehicles, $542 for used vehicles
  and $617 for new leases, up from $749, $532 and $613 in Q2 2025.[^lendingtree2026]
- New/used loan terms averaged 69.5/67.9 months, almost six years. These financing figures exclude other
  ownership costs and do not measure savings from replacing driving with cycling.[^lendingtree2026]

## Financing differences

The reported annual increases were 2.1% for new vehicles, 1.9% for used vehicles and 0.7% for leases.
Average loan amounts were $43,610 new and $27,852 used; lease terms averaged 35.9 months.[^lendingtree2026]

Nonprime borrowers (scores 601–660) had the highest average new payment ($816), largest new loan ($46,079)
and longest new term (75.6 months). Super-prime borrowers (781–850) averaged $741, $41,667 and 64.9 months.
The lowest-score group (300–500) averaged $779 per month for a new vehicle, so the payment gradient is not
monotonic. Different loan amounts and terms mean these comparisons do not isolate a credit-score or
interest-rate effect.[^lendingtree2026]

## Limits

Only LendingTree's reporting was reviewed, not Experian's underlying report. Sample sizes, weighting and
uncertainty are not given. Financed/leased vehicles are not all vehicles or households; no household-income
denominator supports an affordability share. Monthly repayments are cash commitments, not total economic
ownership costs or marginal driving costs. The article does not evaluate relinquishing a car, mode switching
or access to alternatives.[^lendingtree2026]
