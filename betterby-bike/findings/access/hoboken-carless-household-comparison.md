# Hoboken plan reports more carless households than county and region

## In brief

- The Hoboken Green Infrastructure Strategic Plan excerpt reports carless households at 15.7% in Hoboken,
  compared with 13.7% in Hudson County and 12.5% in North Jersey. The observation year is not supplied.[^plan]
- These are household percentages, not transit or cycling shares. The page gives no data methods or measured
  transport-access or green-infrastructure effects.[^plan]

## What the comparison measures

Printed page 7 uses these percentages to explain the importance of transit for Hoboken residents' mobility.
It does not report how those households travel, how frequently they use transit, or whether their journeys are
safe, affordable or reliable. The supplied page does not identify the dataset, define “carless,” specify the
North Jersey regional boundary or give uncertainty. It cannot establish current household conditions.[^plan]

The page separately recommends green infrastructure in flood-prone areas with vulnerable housing communities.
It does not cross-tabulate carlessness with those communities or measure whether that targeting improves their
transportation or flood protection. Its anticipated benefits are planning judgments, not observed effects.[^plan]
