# Amtrak bicycle-access scores depend on capacity data and station proxies

## In brief

- The League's 2026 benchmark identifies 39 of 46 Amtrak lines with carry-on or trainside checked bike
  service. This does not mean every station on those lines accepts bikes; boxed transport is not equivalent.
- Four dual-service lines average 76.75 points, versus 48.60 for 20 carry-on-only and 49.67 for 15
  checked-only lines, as reported. A built-in bonus and state-investment differences prevent causal attribution.
- Disputed Capitol Corridor capacity would change its score from 46 to 83. Scores describe a dated rubric,
  not demonstrated usable access, ridership gains or verified present service.[^amtrak2026]

## What was measured

The framework weights bicycle capacity at 50 points, ticketing/information at 40 and station staffing at 10,
plus bonus/minus adjustments. Capacity was supplied by Amtrak in September 2025; staffing uses March 2026
schedules, selecting the most-stop schedule and counting only U.S. stops on international lines.
Staffing is a proxy, not a station-by-station bicycle eligibility audit: carry-on works without station staff,
while some unstaffed stops have checked-service exceptions.[^amtrak2026]

Dual-service lines receive two bonus points by definition and are well-resourced state-supported corridors.
The report acknowledges that service type cannot be separated from investment in other features. The
reported means imply about 27–28 points difference; the same paragraph's later “23-point gap” is inconsistent.
Page 17's six Good lines also conflict with seven qualifying scores in the p. 16 table. Group means are
reported here, not independently reconstructed statistics.[^amtrak2026]

## Capacity and scope limitations

Appendix UU retains Amtrak's Capitol Corridor capacity despite the corridor operator describing much more
space. Amtrak had not verified that alternative as of July 21, 2026. Using it would raise the hardware score
to its maximum and add a capacity bonus, changing Moderate (46) to Excellent (83). This is a sensitivity
illustration, not a verified correction or an actual service improvement.[^amtrak2026]

The seven lines classed without these bike services are Acela, Adirondack, Auto Train, Berkshire Flyer,
Borealis, Heartland Flyer and Mardi Gras. The snapshot should not be used as current booking advice or
as proof that folded/boxed bicycles are prohibited. No passenger-weighted access, equity or mode-shift
outcome is measured. The PDF retains a draft label and the Source review is partial.
