# Silver Moon Lodge reported high occupancy with few car spaces and bicycle amenities

ULI's 2016 profile describes **Silver Moon Lodge**, an Albuquerque development opened in **November 2014**, with
**154 studio and one-bedroom workforce housing units** and **23 on-site car spaces**. Its property manager reported
**over 95% occupancy** and said parking had not been an issue because many residents relied on bicycles.[^uli2016]

The project used New Mexico Mortgage Finance Authority tax credits. At reporting, eligible annual incomes were capped
at **$26,460 for one person** or **$30,240 for two**, and listed monthly apartment rents were **$661–$708**. Bicycle
provision included a **50-bike storage room**, repair room and resident bike share. Nearby bike routes and trails,
a bus stop and an on-site car-share station supplied multiple alternatives to ownership.[^uli2016]

ULI says fewer car spaces reduced site costs, but gives no savings amount or comparison development. Occupancy and
parking adequacy are a manager's account, not a resident travel survey, parking-use study or estimate of household
transportation costs. The report does not establish that bicycle amenities made the rents affordable; tax-credit
financing and income restrictions are part of this case. These are historical terms, not current eligibility or rents.
