# Maryland mode-shift savings are a reported conditional household scenario

## In brief

- July 2025 America Is All In coverage reports RMI modeling of **over $3,000 per year per average
  Maryland household** in vehicle fuel, maintenance and depreciation savings if state climate and
  mode-shift goals are achieved, not observed household savings.[^moravec2025]
- The article does not give the scenario year, reference-case assumptions or full replacement-travel
  costs. It cannot establish net cash savings, equitable access or the benefit of a bicycle lane.

## Evidence and interpretation

The estimate is attributed to RMI's Smarter MODES calculator in a coalition account of support for
Maryland's Transportation and Climate Alignment Act. Only the article was reviewed, not the calculator
or its underlying analysis. The captured calculator image area is blank; its caption provides no
additional results. The prose does not specify uncertainty, household distribution, the required
amount of driving reduction or how the average was calculated.[^moravec2025]

Depreciation is preserved vehicle value, not necessarily an immediate reduction in cash outlays.
The listed categories do not establish that transit fares, bicycle purchases or implementation costs
were deducted. Achieving modeled goals is an assumption, not a measured response to the proposed
legislation or the executive order described in the article.

## Related evidence

The [US Freedom to Move household scenario](us-freedom-to-move-household-savings.md) also includes
vehicle-value savings, but has a different geography and cumulative horizon. The Maryland annual
figure is neither an independent confirmation of that result nor a quantity to add to it.
See [active commuting and access](../../topics/active-commuting-and-access.md#reduced-driving-scenarios-are-not-observed-access-gains)
for the distinction between modeled savings and maintained access.

[^moravec2025]: Moravec (2025), PDF pp. 7–8, “RMI drove the conversation forward with cost savings
    and policy expertise,” as summarized in the linked Source.
