# Parking-cost scenarios illustrate burdens on car-free households, not observed rent savings

## In brief

- Litman's December 2025 US-dollar scenarios assign lower-income car-free households **$150 per month**
  for one unused required parking space or **$300** for two, about **13% of $1,200 rent** or **21% of $1,400 rent**.
- These are illustrative cost allocations and potential savings, not measured tenant bills or rent reductions
  after removing minimums. They do not establish savings caused by bicycle infrastructure.[^litman]

## Assumptions and denominators

Table 5 assumes one space for a one-bedroom unit and two for a two-bedroom unit, at $150 monthly per space.
With zero vehicles, the whole mandated allocation is treated as excess. Table 6 assumes $3,000 monthly household
income, making these costs **5% and 10% of income**, respectively. Rent shares and income shares are different
measures and should not be substituted for each other.

For higher-cost underground parking, Table 6 assumes $300 per space: one or two unused spaces cost $300 or
$600 monthly, **10% or 20% of the same assumed income**. The author notes that such costs may instead exclude
lower-income households from expensive neighborhoods; these are not observed prevalence estimates.[^litman]

## Limits and interpretation

The comparison removes the assumed cost of unused parking, not a measured policy change holding other housing
conditions fixed. Actual rents depend on market conditions and how savings are passed through. Households may
own vehicles, rent spare spaces, use them for storage, receive housing subsidies or already face reduced mandates.
The report's broader 10–20% housing-cost-saving claim is not an independently estimated average reform effect.

Non-car-free rows in Table 5 contain mismatches between displayed ownership and excess-space calculations;
those rows are not used here. The scenarios support a possible burden mechanism, not a universal affordability
benefit or proof that households can meet their needs without cars.[^litman]
